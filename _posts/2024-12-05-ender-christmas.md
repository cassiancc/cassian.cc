Released another port to Fabric/NeoForge 1.21.x, introducing Ender Christmas Plus! 

This mod adds a unique Christmas texture for the ender chest, as well as an option to enable Christmas textures year-round. 
<!--more-->

The Christmas texture works the same as vanilla Christmas Chest textures, and will be visible only during Christmas.

This port also adds a config option for Christmas Every Day, enabling Christmas Chests every day of the year.

This mod is client-side only, and not required on servers. It is purely visual and does not change any behavior.

This mod is available for Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21 and above.
