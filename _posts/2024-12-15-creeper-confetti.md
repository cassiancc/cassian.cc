Another 1.21 port, it's Creeper Confetti Plus! This mod causes Creepers to explode into confetti (fireworks), which deal damage to players without affecting the landscape. 

<!--more-->

The mod has config options to make the confetti harmless, a chance to play a sound when they explode, and to make the confetti random.

<hr>

This mod is available for Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21 and above.

<https://modrinth.com/mod/creeperconfetti+>
